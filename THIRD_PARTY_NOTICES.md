# Third-party notices

MarkLens vendors browser builds of three libraries so reading works completely offline.
Exact versions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `app/vendor/VERSIONS.json`.

- [Marked 18.0.11](https://www.npmjs.com/package/marked/v/18.0.11), MIT License.
- [DOMPurify 3.4.14](https://www.npmjs.com/package/dompurify/v/3.4.14), Apache-2.0 or MPL-2.0 dual license.
- [highlight.js CDN assets 11.12.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@highlightjs/cdn-assets/v/11.12.0), BSD 3-Clause License.

The complete upstream license texts are stored next to the vendored files in
`app/vendor/` and are distributed in the installer payload.
